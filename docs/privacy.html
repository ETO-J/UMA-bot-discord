Privacy Policy — Bourbon (美浦波旁) Discord Application

Last updated: 2026-10-19

This Privacy Policy explains how the Discord application Bourbon (美浦波旁) ("the App", "we", "us") handles data when you interact with it on Discord or use its web drawing interface at bourbon-draw.cn. The App is operated by its individual developer/operator ("the Operator").

By using the App you agree to the practices described below.

1. What is the App?

The App is a multi-function AI companion bot for Discord (invite-only; it runs only on the servers its Operator has chosen) plus an optional browser-based AI image/video drawing interface at bourbon-draw.cn. The same Discord application powers both; the web interface uses Discord OAuth2 login with the identify scope only.

2. What data does the App process?

2.1 Discord bot

  • Discord identifiers: your user ID, display name/username, avatar, and the IDs of servers and channels in which the App operates.
  • Message content: the text of messages the App can see in the servers/channels where it is enabled. This includes plain-text commands (e.g. /波旁 …, /帮助, /学习 …, /人设列表, /切换人设 …, /今日担当, /识图, /涩图, and the administrator commands /撤回, /长期撤回, /ban, /抓取, /debug, /广播, /清除, /重启), ordinary conversation in channels where the App's chat or moderation features are enabled, and moderation/auto-translation channels configured by the server administrator. On Discord the leading / is optional.
  • Images and attachments: images you send to the App for understanding, image-source lookup (/识图), or images the App sends to you (e.g. /涩图, /今日担当).
  • Conversation memory and logs: recent conversation turns per channel/user session, and operational logs (inbound messages, feature decisions, tool calls, outbound messages) kept by the Operator for the bot's proper functioning and troubleshooting.
  • Knowledge entries: facts users/admins teach with /学习 (and admins with /批量学习), stored in the App's knowledge base.

2.2 Web drawing interface (bourbon-draw.cn)

  • Discord account data after OAuth2 login with the identify scope: user ID, username, avatar. A signed session token is stored in your browser (valid for 7 days).
  • Content you create: prompts, negative prompts, workflow/settings choices, uploaded source images or media, generated images/videos, per-user generation quotas and, where enabled, your media library/asset uploads.
  • Published media: when the asset-publish feature is enabled, the thumbnail/attachment of a published work may be posted to a configured Discord channel by the Operator's bot.

3. Why is this data processed?

  • Conversational AI: answering questions and keeping coherent context; deciding whether to take part in free-form channel conversation; image understanding.
  • Knowledge base: grounding answers in server-specific facts taught to the App.
  • Commands and utilities: persona switching, image-source lookup, Pixiv image lookup, daily random character image, scheduled greetings, help.
  • Moderation and safety: trap-channel spam detection, user blacklists, message removal and ban commands used by administrators.
  • Administration: channel-history archiving (/抓取), restart/clear/debug commands.
  • Web drawing: signing you in, remembering your settings/quotas, generating media and keeping your generation history/assets.

4. Where is the data stored and retained?

  • Everything the bot stores is stored locally on the Operator's own private server/ machine — conversation sessions (DeepSeek Harness session store), the knowledge base (FAISS index and text files), activity/console/sidecar logs, and archives produced by the administrator-only /抓取 command. Nothing in this list is hosted by a third party on the Operator's behalf.
  • Conversation sessions are retained to provide memory. Server administrators can clear a session with /清除, which starts a fresh session generation; the previous local session log remains on the Operator's disk until the Operator removes it, but it is no longer used as active memory. Knowledge entries can be removed with /删除知识. Users may contact the Operator (section 9) to request deletion.
  • The web interface stores user settings, quotas, task queues and generated media in the Operator's local databases and on its local disks. Generated media and uploaded assets are retained until you or the Operator deletes them.
  • Logs are kept for operations/security and are deleted by the Operator from time to time or on request. There is no fixed automatic retention period.

5. Do we share or transfer your data?

  • We never sell or rent your data, and we do not share message content with third parties for advertising or profiling.
  • To provide a response, your prompt/message may be transmitted transiently to the AI providers the Operator uses (e.g. DeepSeek's chat API for conversation and vision; the search API used by the harness's web-search tool; SauceNAO for image-source lookup; the Pixiv API for image lookup; a locally hosted ComfyUI for image/video generation). These services process the data only to answer that request. The Operator does not use your content to train any machine-learning model.
  • The web interface's login flow talks only to Discord's OAuth2 endpoints.

6. How is the data protected?

  • The App runs on infrastructure controlled by the Operator; access to credentials, databases and logs is limited to the Operator.
  • Sensitive outbound content is inspected before being sent to Discord; known credentials and credential-shaped strings are replaced rather than published.
  • Autonomous AI operations are confined to a dedicated workspace on the Operator's machine.
  • Moderation actions (purge/ban) and archive commands are available only to configured administrator IDs and require the corresponding Discord permissions.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Remove or disable the App: ask a server administrator to remove the App, or disable its features in a channel (server configuration). Users do not need to interact with the App.
  • Blacklisted users: users blacklisted by the Operator are never answered and their messages are not stored as memory.
  • Deletion requests: contact the Operator (section 9) to request deletion of your data. Administrators can clear conversation memory on demand (/清除), and remove knowledge entries (/删除知识).
  • Web interface: you can stop using it at any time and ask the Operator to delete your account data, settings, quotas and generated media.

8. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13+ (or the minimum age required by Discord's Terms of Service in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age.
